Outfits

2026 — a shopping list, and 53 outfits I haven't worn yet

I gave Claude three years of my camera roll and asked it, more or less, how to dress. It came back with a long list of opinions and a shopping list. This page is the shopping list, plus what the outfits would look like on me.

None of the photos here are real. The outfits were generated with GPT Image from one reference photo of me and cutouts of clothes I actually own. The product pictures are generated too, so they show the kind of thing I'm after, not the exact item behind the link. The links are where I'd start looking, and the prices are rough.
