Model Notes

This calculator uses a first-order radial-flux PMSM estimate. It computes pole flux from airgap area, links it through the series turns per phase, derives Kt, then uses the SI Kt/Kv relationship. Resistance is estimated from mean turn length and copper area. Continuous torque is set by the copper-loss thermal budget you enter.

It does not model slot saturation, tooth geometry, leakage, magnet thickness, skew, detailed end turns, demagnetization, eddy loss, iron loss, or controller voltage limits with high fidelity. Treat anything within about +/-30 percent as directionally useful, not final design data.
