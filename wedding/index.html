How does ranked choice work?

For each role, rank as many people as you like, favourite first. Everyone's first choice is counted. If nobody has more than half, the last-place candidate is knocked out and each of their ballots moves to its next choice. Repeat until someone has a majority. Your vote is never wasted: if your first pick is out, your second one counts. Everything saves automatically and every device sees the same results within a few seconds.
